| 052 | [Claim before you charge](052-claim-before-you-charge.md) | Accepted | 2026-07-13 | Two code paths take money — `POST /payments/capture` and the ship-triggered capture inside `POST /fulfillments/:id/ship` — and **both checked `payment.status === AUTHORIZED` on an UNLOCKED read, called the gateway, and only then opened a transaction and re-read.** The precondition that requires the lock was verified **after the money had already moved.** Two callers pass that check at the same instant, both charge the processor, and the loser throws `PAYMENT_INVALID_STATUS_TRANSITION` and rolls back: the database records one capture and one clean 409, and **a rollback cannot un-call a payment gateway.** The window is not narrow — **it is the width of a round-trip to a payment processor**, because the gateway call is *what sits inside it*.
